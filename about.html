WHY CIVICBIRD

Less running around.
More room to live.

CivicBird is being built around a simple idea: when a task can lawfully be handled by a representative, people should have a dependable way to request help.

Our mission

We help busy people save time on eligible everyday errands by connecting them with a Bird—a service assistant who follows the rules, respects people's information and communicates clearly.

What “Bird” means

A Bird is a CivicBird service assistant assigned to an accepted task. Birds are not government employees and do not have special authority. Any representation must be permitted by the relevant office and supported by appropriate authorisation.

Where we operate

Our initial service area is Bhagalpur, Bihar. Availability depends on the specific task and office rules.

What we promise

  • Clear fees before you confirm
  • Respectful, lawful service
  • Honest status updates without promises of approval
  • Careful handling of documents and personal information
CivicBird is an independent private assistance service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of any government department.

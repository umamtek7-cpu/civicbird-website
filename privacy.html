What this prototype collects

The pages are static and do not have a server or database that receives or stores form submissions. Form content stays in your browser until you copy it or choose to share it using another app.

Do not submit sensitive data

Do not enter Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, bank account information, passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or scans of identity documents into this prototype.

External services

If you choose to open WhatsApp or another external service, that service's own terms and privacy practices apply.

Before launch

Before enabling real bookings, CivicBird should publish an accurate privacy policy naming the data controller, purpose of collection, legal basis where applicable, retention periods, access controls, grievance contact and how users can request correction or deletion. A secure backend and restricted admin access must be configured.

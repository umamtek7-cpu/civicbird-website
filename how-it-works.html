Four steps from
request to update.

You stay informed. Our team coordinates the eligible errand.

01

Tell us what you need

Select an office and describe the task through the booking form. Do not upload sensitive identity documents on this static website.

02

We check eligibility and price

Our team checks whether a representative is allowed to handle the task, what documents are needed, and whether extra official fees apply.

03

Choose how to pay

Once a booking process is configured, choose advance online payment through a secure payment provider or pay after work, when offered. Never send card details or OTPs to a Bird.

04

A Bird is assigned

Our team manually assigns a Bird. We share progress and a completion update. Government approval and processing times remain outside CivicBird's control.

Current website status: This is a static frontend prototype. Booking requests are not yet stored or delivered to an admin automatically, and online payment is not connected. Use the form's copy/WhatsApp options only after the contact number is configured.

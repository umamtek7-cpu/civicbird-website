Frequently asked questions

Can a Bird complete every government-office task?

No. Some tasks require the customer to appear personally, provide biometrics, sign in person or satisfy other statutory requirements. We accept only tasks where representation is permitted.

Is ₹354 the full cost?

₹354 is the standard service fee including 18% GST, based on the pricing supplied by the business owner. Official fees, postage, printing, travel or other agreed charges may be extra and must be disclosed before confirmation.

Can you guarantee government approval?

No. Decisions and processing times belong to the relevant office. CivicBird can help with eligible errands but cannot guarantee outcomes.

How do I track my booking?

Booking tracking is not yet connected in this static prototype. A backend and admin workflow are required before live tracking can be offered.

How can I complain?

Once contact details are configured, you can use the official support channel. Keep your booking reference and receipts, but do not send passwords or UPI PINs.

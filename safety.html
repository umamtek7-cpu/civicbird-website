Our service standards are designed to protect customers, Birds and the integrity of official processes.

Identity & authorisation

Customers should verify a Bird's identity through the official CivicBird contact channel before handing over documents or money. Written authorisation may be required. Never provide an OTP, password, PIN or biometric data to a Bird.

Lawful work only

We do not offer bribes, influence-peddling, false documents, impersonation, queue-jumping, or any attempt to bypass government rules. If personal attendance, biometrics, an original signature or a statutory declaration is required, the customer must follow the office's rules.

Document privacy

Share only information needed for a confirmed task and only through an approved channel. This public GitHub Pages website does not provide secure document upload, customer accounts or private booking storage. Do not submit Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or other sensitive documents through this prototype.

Payments

Pay only through a payment method officially confirmed by CivicBird. Never disclose card details, UPI PINs or OTPs. Official government fees are separate from CivicBird's service fee and should be supported by official receipts where applicable.

Limits of service

CivicBird cannot guarantee approval, processing time or a particular decision by any government office. Tasks may be declined if representation is not permitted or requirements are unclear.
